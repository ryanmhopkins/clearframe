PHOTO DELIVERY CHECKLIST
ClearFrame | https://getclearframe.app/batch-remove-photo-metadata#delivery-checklist

Use on the downloaded delivery copies. Keep originals separate.
For mixed batches, repeat for each camera, format, and export preset.

[ ] Extract the delivery ZIP, if used, and open the cleaned copies.
    Check rotation, colors, transparency, and animation where relevant.
    If appearance changed, revisit orientation and color-profile choices.

[ ] Re-scan a downloaded copy and review Photo details and listed blocks.
    Check the location, capture dates, camera identifiers, author fields,
    and editing notes you intended to remove.

[ ] Confirm author, copyright, and captions you intended to keep.
    JPEG, PNG, WebP, and GIF cleanup removes whole blocks, not individual
    tags. GPS and copyright can share an EXIF block. Use a tag-level
    editor if you must keep selected tags within the same removed block.

[ ] Review filenames, folder names, ZIP names, and visible photo content.
    Filesystem dates are separate from embedded capture dates.

[ ] Review warnings and retained data before sending. Keep originals.
    RAW and extended formats retain some rendering data. Missing fields
    in a scan do not prove every proprietary or hidden identifier is gone.
    Metadata cleanup does not guarantee anonymity.
