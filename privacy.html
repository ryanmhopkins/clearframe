Privacy policy

This page describes what the ClearFrame website does with information. It is written in plain language for a personal project. It is not a lawyer-drafted policy.

Who publishes this site

ClearFrame is published by Ryan Hopkins as a personal project. There is no separate company behind it. The contact address is ryan@ryanmhopkins.com, which is the public email on ryanmhopkins.com.

Photos you choose

Scanning and cleaning happen in your browser. The photo files you select are not uploaded to this site or to any other server by this page. Cleaned copies and ZIP downloads are created in the browser and saved by your browser.

ClearFrame does not have accounts, and it does not ask for your name, email, or payment details to use the tool.

What this site downloads

Loading ClearFrame downloads the page, its scripts, its styles, and its fonts from this website. HEIC, HEIF, AVIF, TIFF, and RAW photos also cause the browser to download a photo engine of about 25 MB from this same website the first time you need it. That file is the engine, not your photo. JPEG, PNG, WebP, and GIF cleaning does not download that engine.

Scripts, fonts, and the photo engine are part of this site. The page does not load them from Google Fonts, a public script CDN, or another company’s servers.

The home page section How your privacy works explains how to confirm this in your browser’s network tab.

What the host can see

Opening this website connects to the host that serves it. The site is currently hosted on Vercel at clear-frame-app.vercel.app. Like other websites, that connection can include your IP address, the time, your browser’s user agent, and the address of the page you requested. Your photos are not part of those requests.

This project’s files do not configure or read those host logs, and this page cannot turn them off. How long the host keeps them is the host’s setting, not something stated here because this repository does not control it.

Cookies, analytics, and ads

The scripts in this project do not set cookies, and the pages do not include analytics or advertising. There is no third-party tracker in these files.

The X link

The footer link “Created by Ryan” goes to an X profile (x.com/rmhopkins) only if you click it. The page does not embed X. A click does not include your photos. Your browser will then be talking to X under X’s own policy.

Children

ClearFrame is a general photo tool. It is not directed at children, and it does not offer accounts or a place to send personal details. It also does not receive the photos you open.

Changes

This policy can be updated when the site changes. The date at the top is when this wording was last updated.

Contact

Privacy questions and corrections: ryan@ryanmhopkins.com. See the contact page.
