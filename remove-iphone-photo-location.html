Your iPhone photos can carry GPS coordinates, dates, and camera details. ClearFrame lets you scan that metadata and remove what you choose before sharing a copy.

Choose photos to clean

Runs in your browser. Photos stay on-device and are not uploaded for scanning or cleaning. No account needed for the free cleaner.

How to remove location from an iPhone photo

  1. Open the photo cleaner and choose your HEIC, HEIF, or JPEG photos.
  2. Review the detected metadata and removal choices. Select the GPS/location option where available. Some options remove a whole EXIF block, including related tags.
  3. Download a cleaned copy. Scan that copy again to check the remaining metadata before sharing it.

Your original stays unchanged. The downloaded copy keeps its original format without resizing or recompressing the image.

HEIC works here, even without a preview

HEIC and HEIF are included in the free cleaner. Your browser may not show a preview, but you can still review and clean a supported file. On first use, the browser downloads the photo engine from this site; your photo stays on your device.

Removing GPS is one part of sharing carefully

Metadata cleaning is not forensic anonymity. A street sign, landmark, face, or other visible detail can still reveal information. ClearFrame does not remove visible content or promise to find every piece of identifying data. Review the cleaned copy and its contents before you share.
